In our rapidly changing world, agriculture plays a crucial role in feeding a growing global population. To meet increasing food demands, farmers face the challenge of enhancing productivity while considering sustainability. This review explores how new technologies, like digital farming and precision tools, are transforming agriculture. It examines studies that discuss the adoption of smart farming techniques, the impact of data-driven approaches, and the evolving relationship between technology, sustainability, and the farmer's experience.

Smart farming according to Santiteerakul, S., Sopadang, A., et.al., (2020), is a management strategy that harnesses modern technology to enhance both the quantity and quality of

agricultural yields. It entails blending information and communication technologies with machinery, sensors, actuators, and network devices within agricultural production systems.
