CropIntel Team Manpower Requirements

Person Requirements
Project Manager 5+ years of experience in project management, preferably in the tech or agriculture sectors. Strong leadership and communication skills are essential.
Software Developer Proficiency in programming languages such as Python and Java. Experience with AI and machine learning is preferred, along with familiarity with agricultural applications.
Data Scientist Expertise in data analysis, statistical modeling, and machine learning algorithms. Proficient in tools like R or Python, with a focus on agricultural data analytics.
Marketing Specialist Experience in digital marketing strategies, particularly within the agricultural sector. Strong understanding of social media and SEO tactics to engage farmers and buyers effectively.
Customer Support Agent Excellent communication skills and a background in customer service. Ability to assist users with technical issues and provide insights into product features, preferably with experience in agricultural or tech support.
